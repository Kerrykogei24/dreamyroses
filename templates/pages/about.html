About us

A one-woman studio for pages that belong to somebody.

Dreamy Roses is run by Dreamy Roses from Nairobi. It started with one drawing made as a gift, and the look on the face of the person who opened it.

Most coloring books are full of strangers. The pages here are not. Miriam works from the photographs you already have on your phone — the graduation gown, the back seat of the car, the afternoon by the pool — and redraws each one as line art clean enough to colour and close enough to recognise.

Every page is drawn to order. Nothing is traced from stock, and nothing is resold to anyone else. When your book is done, the drawings are yours.

Alongside the personalised work, the studio publishes ready-made digital sets in the Dreamy Roses style — anime portraits, couples, florals — for anyone who just wants something beautiful to sit down with.
